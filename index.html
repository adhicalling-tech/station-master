      Station Master

      Route every train into the station and back out. Give each train its own platform type for full points, and never let two trains meet.

      How to play

      1. Tap a train waiting at the outer signal, or one still on its way: if you clear its signal early it runs straight through without stopping.
      2. Tap the platform it should use.
      3. Tap a platform, or a siding to hold it.
      4. Tap the flashing signal to send it in.
      5. When boarding ends, tap the train, then its exit signal.

      Rules

      • Only one train can use each end's junction at a time.
      • A signal will not clear a route that conflicts with another train.
      • Sidings hold a train out of the way while you wait for a platform. There is one siding on each side. It holds one train and then runs straight into the platforms beside it (the west siding to the upper island, PF 1 and PF 2; the east siding to the lower island, PF 3 and PF 4), so a parked train can go directly to one of those platforms. Non-stop trains never use sidings. A train that cannot come in yet waits outside, and it costs points (more for express and non-stop trains) and arrives already late.
      • The timetable boards at each end show which trains are due, in how many seconds, and which are about to leave. Use them to plan: for example park a slow train in a siding so an express due in a few seconds can come straight in. Each train also has a planned platform (for example PF2) on the board: bring it there for bonus points and happier passengers. Tap a train and its row lights up on the board. If you send it to another platform, the board updates and an announcement appears at the top. You can switch the boards off on this page.
      • Some trains run late ("late 8s") or early ("early 4s"), shown on the boards and on the train. Passengers on a late train are already unhappy, but if you send it in promptly (within 3 seconds at the signal) and out again as soon as it has finished boarding (within 3 seconds), it recovers: its satisfaction is restored, you earn a bonus and its efficiency gets a lift. An early train has time in hand and can wait, so park it in a siding if a late train needs the way.
      • Priority: express, local, goods.
      • Priority: non-stop, express, local, goods.
      • From time to time a passenger track closes for maintenance for a short while. You get a 20-second warning first (it flashes); in the last 6 seconds no new train can use it, and it stays closed until it reopens.
      • At the Junction a whole island platform (both its tracks) can also close for maintenance, with a 25-second warning. The through lines and the goods line never close, and one passenger track always stays open.
      • Keep a safe gap: trains on the same line must stay about three carriage lengths apart. A faster train will slow down behind a slower one automatically, but if you cleared it too close you lose points. Leave a train time to get clear before sending the next one.
      • Every train ends with a satisfaction score out of 100: it drops for a wrong platform, a long wait outside, a late departure or a too-close clearance. Your round's satisfaction is the average, shown at the top of the screen as "happy".
      • Pass a round with at least 90% satisfaction to earn bonus points: 50 at 90%, rising steadily to 200 at 100%.
      • From the Junction a branch line leaves the east side. A train from the west with a destination arrow ("→ Branch") must leave by the branch; the others go along the main line. A branch train leaves through the branch signal (the one with the amber arrow); every other train leaves through its platform's exit signal. Tap the departing train, then the exit signal for the way it should go. With Exit guidance on (Info page, the default) the right line glows, the right signal flashes and a wrong signal is refused with a hint. With guidance off nothing flashes, and a wrong signal sends the train the wrong way and costs points.
      • The journey ends at round 30 (the Daily challenge is a short one of five rounds, a different five each day). Pass the last round to see your final rank and share your score with a friend.
      • Efficiency is a second score out of 100, shown at the top as "efficient". A train loses efficiency when it waits outside while a right platform and a safe route were free, and when it sits at a platform after boarding is done (each after a 2.5 second grace). Reach 80% to earn bonus points: 40 at 80%, rising steadily to 150 at 100%.
      • From round 6 one VIP train (purple with gold trim, marked with a star) sometimes arrives, with a warning first. A stopping VIP: clear it quickly and send it on time. From round 7 it can also be a non-stop VIP: clear it onto its through line at once and never slow it. Either way the bonus is big.
      • After every round you get a score report listing each mistake and how the points were made.
      • Each round is a fixed set of trains (shown below the track). Handle every one to finish the round. Pass it to move on; the station gets bigger as you go.
      • Each train must stay its full time: local 6 s, goods 9 s. Then you get a free buffer (4 and 6 s). Past that, points drain.
      • From round 4 some expresses are Limited: a different colour (magenta, or yellow in colour-blind mode) with a shorter stop (2.5 s) and less patience. Clear one in quickly and send it on as soon as boarding ends, and you earn a bonus.
      • Each train must stay its full time: express 4 s, local 6 s, goods 9 s. Then you get a free buffer (3, 4 and 6 s). Past that, points drain.
